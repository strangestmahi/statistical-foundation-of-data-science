# Practical 3: Statistical Analysis and Data Visualization using Python

## Aim
To perform statistical analysis and data visualization on the Teaching Ratings dataset using Python.

## Libraries Used
- **Pandas:** For data loading, filtering and statistical analysis.
- **Matplotlib:** For creating graphs and visualizations.

## Dataset
The Teaching Ratings dataset is obtained from the IBM Skills Network cloud storage. It contains information about 463 professors, including their age, gender, minority status, tenure, beauty scores and evaluation scores.

## Description

The following operations were performed:

1. Imported Pandas and Matplotlib libraries.
2. Loaded the Teaching Ratings dataset from an online CSV file.
3. Displayed the column names of the dataset.
4. Filtered the data to select visible minority professors.
5. Calculated the percentage of minority professors who are tenured.
6. Used cross-tabulation to compare tenure percentages between minority and non-minority professors.
7. Calculated the mean and standard deviation of professors' ages based on tenure status.
8. Created a histogram to show the distribution of professors' ages.
9. Calculated the number of male and female professors.
10. Created vertical and horizontal bar graphs to display gender distribution.
11. Calculated the median evaluation score of tenured professors.

## Important Results

- Total professors: **463**
- Minority professors who are tenured: **84.375%**
- Non-minority professors who are tenured: **76.94%**
- Mean age of untenured professors: **50.19 years**
- Mean age of tenured professors: **47.85 years**
- Male professors: **268**
- Female professors: **195**
- Median evaluation score of tenured professors: **4.0**
